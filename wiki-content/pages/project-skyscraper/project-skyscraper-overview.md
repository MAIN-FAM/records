# Project Skyscraper

[TOC]

## What Is It?

Project Skyscraper is an alternate reality game (ARG) that emerged within the
No Man's Sky community in early 2026. A website at project-skyscraper.com began
publishing what appeared to be corrupted system logs from an entity calling
itself **The Architect**.

> [!NOTE]
> "Project Skyscraper" was also the internal codename used during early
> development of No Man's Sky at Hello Games, as well as "skyscraper" being the name of the game engine.

## Timeline

The site was first noticed in late March 2026, posting cryptic entries at
regular intervals. The ETARC community moderators surfaced it publicly on
**May 15, 2026**. Within days, the fiction named its own operators as
part of the story, linking directly back to the ETARC discussion thread.

## How It Works

The Architect & System publish memory blocs, security logs, and diagnostic reports
to the website. The community solves ciphers and puzzles embedded in these
posts. Solving them visibly changes the site - new pages appear, text
mutates, things are added and removed, and the narrative advances.

> [!TIP]
> The ARG is interactive. Community solutions directly affect what
> The Architect and System publish next.

## Key Elements

- **The Architect** - the entity posting logs and communicating with Operators
- **System** - the AI infrastructure of relatively unknown origin communicating with Operators
- **Operators** - the players engaging with the ARG
- **Memory Blocs** - timestamped entries that encode recoverable/recovered/injected memories
- **TRACE** - the in-universe communication platform that is ETARC

## Community Involvement

The investigation community spans NMScord, ETARC, NMS France Discord, Galactic Hub, and many other communities. Operators have conducted real-world field verifications in London, and virtual field verifications in Brussels, Paris and Ottawa - confirming "structural / temporal anomalies" that the fiction predicted.

> [!WARNING]
> The ARG blends fiction with reality. Always verify information through
> community channels before treating it as canonical.
